- **Word count target:** 2,500-4,000 words for comparison/listicle pieces
- **Tutorial pieces:** 1,500-2,500 words with screenshots/GIFs
- **Update frequency:** Major comparison articles should be refreshed monthly
- **Internal linking:** Every article links to 2-3 other articles in the hub

### Monetization Angles
- **Affiliate links:** Cursor, Midjourney, Jasper, Copy.ai, Writesonic all have programs
- **Display ads:** Free tool listicles will drive highest pageview traffic
- **Sponsored content:** B2B tools (ClickUp, Notion) pay for placements

### Keyword Difficulty Assessment
- 🟢 Low competition: Claude Code, FLUX Kontext, Kling tutorials, Sora alternatives

2. **Pricing calculators** — interactive tools comparing monthly costs
3. **Decision flowcharts** — "Which AI tool is right for you?" visual guides
4. **Video companions** — embed short demo videos for each tool reviewed
